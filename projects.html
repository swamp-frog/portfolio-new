"On the Balcony of Orange Street"

This two part book is my attempt at telling the story of the lost and found photographs of a dutch engineer in Suriname. The book invites the readers to take in the period scenery before slowly revealing the village where it all took place. Originally captured in the 1900s, the photographs have been collected from the Rijks Museum archive and repurposed into this book.

"Onion Creek, Austin, Texas"

If you moved to Onion Creek, Austin, Texas, today, and lived there for the rest of your life, would you be certain to say, on your death bed, that your neighbour 5 streets down changed his bathroom sink in 2019? If you owned this book, you would. Using very specific data from the city of Austin, Texas, this book is a collection of entries made to give readers a unique and personal view of a neighbourhood in the United States.

"Without a Trace"

Re-print of the discontinued "How to disappear in China without a trace" book by Susanne Bürner. This adaptation puts a spin on the function of the book. The pages have been perforated to allow the evader to rip them out one by one. Designed together with Jonas Rimersma.

"Boombox Introspective"

There is no better way to change the atmosphere than setting down a Boombox. What's more characteristic of 21st century ingenuity than making your own devices out of discarded equipment? Duchamp started in 1913 and with so much junk still around, making readymades is still relevant today. Mass production hasn't provided us with the endless supply of futuristic products we were promised and yet, this abundance of junk offers unlimited inspiration. A speaker like this could've been built decades ago, and it's still better than anything on AliExpress. But would it have?
